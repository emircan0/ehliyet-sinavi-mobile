Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 1, 2026

Ehliyet Hocam ("Application") places the highest priority on the privacy and security of your personal data.

1. Data Controller

The Ehliyet Hocam application is operated by independent developer **Emircan Mert**. Contact: support@ehliyethocam.com

2. Collected Data

For registration, your e-mail address, user name, exam success statistics, wrong answers, and favorite content are collected. Additionally, an anonymous device ID is processed for the purpose of sending notifications.

3. Data Processing Purposes

Your data is used to create personalized study programs, perform error analysis with the 'AI Tutor' feature, and provide progress charts.

4. Data Sharing

Your data is never sold to advertising companies. Supabase is used for secure storage and Apple infrastructure is used for login processes.

5. User Rights

Under KVKK and GDPR, you have the right to access, correct, or delete your data. You can delete all your data by closing your account within the application.
