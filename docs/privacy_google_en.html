Privacy Policy (Google Play)

Last Updated: April 16, 2026

At Ehliyet Hocam: Akıllı Sınav, we prioritize the privacy and security of your personal data. This policy transparently explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and how we protect it.

1. Data Controller and Scope

All data collected through the Ehliyet Hocam: Akıllı Sınav application is processed in full compliance with relevant data protection regulations and legislation. As the data controller, we guarantee that we only collect the information necessary to provide you with a superior experience.

2. Processed Data and Collection Method

To personalize your experience, we process the following information:

  • Account Information: Name and email address provided during registration.
  • Usage Data: Exam results, mistakes made, favorite questions, and study statistics.
  • Device Information: We may collect non-identifiable information about your device to ensure technical compatibility.

3. Data Processing Purposes and AI Analysis

Your performance data is analyzed by our 'AI Tutor' feature. Our goal is to identify your weak subjects using advanced technological approaches and offer a customized study plan to increase your success rate. All AI processes utilize anonymous data sets to ensure your profile security is maintained.

4. Advertising and Analytics

We may use your Google Advertising ID to provide personalized advertisements and analyze traffic to improve our services. You can reset these identifiers or opt-out of personalized ads through your device settings.

5. Third-Party Service Providers

We work with trusted third-party services to ensure the technical stability and functionality of our application. These providers have access to your information only to perform these tasks on our behalf and are obligated not to disclose or use it for any other purpose:

  • Google Play Services (Authentication & Analytics)
  • Supabase (Encrypted Database & Storage)
  • RevenueCat (Subscription Management)

6. Children's Privacy

Our services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. If we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

7. User Rights and Data Deletion

The control of your data is entirely in your hands. You can permanently delete all your digital footprint and account information by using the 'Delete My Account' option in your profile settings. You can also contact us at any time to request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data.

Ehliyet Hocam: Akıllı Sınav is designed to support driver candidates' education. Your safety and privacy are our top priorities.
